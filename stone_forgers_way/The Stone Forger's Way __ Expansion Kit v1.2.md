## 1. The New Archetypes :: Definitions & DNA

This expansion introduces six "Frictional Reality Archetypes" that bridge the gap between ancient wisdom and future technology. They are not just roles but states of consciousness that practitioners move through.

**Visual Philosophy: The Nameless Vessel**
All archetypes are presented as "Nameless/Faceless" figures—silhouettes, obscured faces, or symbolic forms. This allows the user to project their own identity into the archetype, rather than seeing a specific character. They are vessels for the user's experience.

### 1.1 The Jade Hunter
**The Seeker of Truth**

*   **Simple Definition:** The one who sees the code beneath the reality.
*   **The Experience:** You feel a constant dissonance between what is shown and what is true. You are often the "hacker" of systems, finding shortcuts or hidden paths. You carry the burden of knowing too much but not always knowing how to act on it.
*   **The Insight:** Your gift is **Discernment**. You don't just carry stones; you analyze their composition. You are the system architect of the Stone Forger's world.
*   **Visual Signature:** A hooded, faceless silhouette examining a floating jade-green cubic stone. The figure is a vessel of focus, obscured in shadow, revealing only the digital wireframe projection of the stone.
*   **Core Practice:** *The Code Audit* — Identifying the underlying belief structure of a stone before picking it up.

### 1.2 The Invitation
**The Opener of Ways**

*   **Simple Definition:** The one who creates space for others to enter.
*   **The Experience:** You are the host, the gatekeeper, the welcomer. You often feel the weight of others' comfort and belonging. Your stones are often the needs and expectations of those you invite in.
*   **The Insight:** Your gift is **Hospitality** (in the ancient sense). You create the Container where forging can happen. You are the threshold guardian who ensures safety.
*   **Visual Signature:** A welcoming figure with obscured features, standing on a path of floating square tiles. They extend an open hand, not as a person, but as the embodiment of the gesture itself.
*   **Core Practice:** *The Threshold Breath* — Consciously opening and closing the energetic gate of your attention.

### 1.3 The Stone Keeper
**The Guardian of Lineage**

*   **Simple Definition:** The one who preserves what must not be lost.
*   **The Experience:** You feel the weight of history, ancestry, and legacy. You carry stones not for yourself, but for those who came before and those who will come after. You struggle with releasing anything that feels "sacred" or "important."
*   **The Insight:** Your gift is **Stewardship**. You ensure the continuity of wisdom. You are the librarian of the Stone Forger's archives.
*   **Visual Signature:** An ageless, faceless guardian standing in a stylized archive. They hold a glowing stone with reverence, their form wrapped in timeless, layered robes that suggest neither past nor future, but permanence.
*   **Core Practice:** *The Lineage Check* — Asking "Is this stone mine, or does it belong to the ancestors/future?"

### 1.4 The Stone Breaker
**The Disruptor of Patterns**

*   **Simple Definition:** The one who cracks open the calcified to reveal the light.
*   **The Experience:** You feel an intense energy to change, fix, or destroy what isn't working. You are often labeled as "intense" or "disruptive." You carry the burden of necessary destruction.
*   **The Insight:** Your gift is **Liberation**. You release the energy trapped in old forms. You are the catalyst of the Stone Forger's evolution.
*   **Visual Signature:** A dynamic, dark silhouette in a powerful martial stance, striking a stone block. The figure is pure kinetic energy, devoid of industrial gear or specific identity—a force of nature cracking the shell.
*   **Core Practice:** *The Impact Strike* — Channeling frustration into a single, focused action of transformation.

### 1.5 The Stone Caller
**The Summoner of Reality**

*   **Simple Definition:** The one who speaks stones into existence.
*   **The Experience:** You realize that your words and thoughts instantly manifest as obligations or creations. You struggle with the weight of your own creative power. You often feel overwhelmed by the sheer volume of what you've "called" in.
*   **The Insight:** Your gift is **Manifestation**. You understand the vibrational nature of stones. You are the bard/magician of the Stone Forger's world.
*   **Visual Signature:** A creative silhouette standing with arms raised, summoning cubic stones from the ground. The figure is an active channel, their identity secondary to the act of creation itself.
*   **Core Practice:** *The Silence Before Speech* — Pausing to verify alignment before speaking a new stone into the world.

### 1.6 The Stone Witness
**The Observer of Chaos**

*   **Simple Definition:** The one who sees without interfering.
*   **The Experience:** You feel a profound detachment that can be mistaken for apathy. You see the chaos of others' stone-throwing and carrying but choose not to engage. You carry the burden of "doing nothing" while seeing everything.
*   **The Insight:** Your gift is **Presence**. You hold the frequency of calm that allows others to find their own way. You are the mirror of the Stone Forger's truth.
*   **Visual Signature:** A grounded, faceless void standing perfectly still amidst a scene of motion. They are not investigating; they are *anchoring*. A pillar of observation in a chaotic world.
*   **Core Practice:** *The Anchored Gaze* — Watching a stone (problem/situation) without trying to fix, move, or judge it.

## 2. Archetype Quiz Integration Logic

To integrate these into the existing quiz, we add a **Secondary Layer** or "Sub-Class" logic. The primary quiz identifies the Core State (Carrier, Thrower, Forger). The new questions refine this into the specific Frictional Reality Archetype.

**Logic Flow:**
1.  **Core Quiz:** Determines Carrier, Thrower, or Forger.
2.  **Expansion Branch:** "Let's go deeper. How do you relate to the stones you carry?"

**New Question Set (Sample):**

*   **Q1: When you encounter a heavy stone (obligation), what is your first instinct?**
    *   A: To analyze why it exists and how it was made. *(Points to Jade Hunter)*
    *   B: To welcome it in and find it a place. *(Points to Invitation)*
    *   C: To check if it belongs to my history or legacy. *(Points to Stone Keeper)*
    *   D: To break it open and see what's inside. *(Points to Stone Breaker)*
    *   E: To realize I asked for this, even unconsciously. *(Points to Stone Caller)*
    *   F: To watch it closely before touching it. *(Points to Stone Witness)*

*   **Q2: What is your relationship to the past/tradition?**
    *   A: It's a code to be rewritten. *(Jade Hunter)*
    *   B: It's a home to be shared. *(Invitation)*
    *   C: It's a treasure to be guarded. *(Stone Keeper)*
    *   D: It's a shell to be cracked. *(Stone Breaker)*
    *   E: It's a story I'm retelling. *(Stone Caller)*
    *   F: It's a river I'm watching. *(Stone Witness)*

**Mapping:**
*   **Jade Hunter** often evolves from *Conscious Forger* or *Stone Carrier* (intellectual burden).
*   **Invitation** often evolves from *Stone Carrier* (emotional burden).
*   **Stone Keeper** often evolves from *Stone Carrier* (lineage burden).
*   **Stone Breaker** often evolves from *Stone Thrower* (reactive energy channeled).
*   **Stone Caller** often evolves from *Stone Thrower* (creative chaos).
*   **Stone Witness** often evolves from *Conscious Forger* (mastery of detachment).

## 3. The "What's Next" Portal :: Progression Paths


Once a user identifies their Expanded Archetype, they receive a specific **Progression Path**.

### Path of the Code (Jade Hunter)
*   **Goal:** Move from analyzing to rewriting.
*   **Tool:** *The System Override* (Journaling prompt to rewrite a limiting belief).
*   **Glossary Lens:** Focus on "Structure," "Pattern," "Algorithm."

### Path of the Threshold (Invitation)
*   **Goal:** Move from over-giving to selective opening.
*   **Tool:** *The Gatekeeper's Key* (Visualization of locking/unlocking your energy).
*   **Glossary Lens:** Focus on "Container," "Boundary," "Hospitality."

### Path of the Archive (Stone Keeper)
*   **Goal:** Move from hoarding to curating.
*   **Tool:** *The Legacy Filter* (Ritual to return stones to ancestors).
*   **Glossary Lens:** Focus on "Lineage," "Stewardship," "Memory."

### Path of the Hammer (Stone Breaker)
*   **Goal:** Move from destruction to sculpting.
*   **Tool:** *The Chisel Strike* (Practice of small, precise changes vs. explosions).
*   **Glossary Lens:** Focus on "Transformation," "Release," "Impact."

### Path of the Voice (Stone Caller)
*   **Goal:** Move from accidental summoning to intentional casting.
*   **Tool:** *The True Name* (Practice of naming desires precisely).
*   **Glossary Lens:** Focus on "Manifestation," "Vibration," "Word."

### Path of the Mirror (Stone Witness)
*   **Goal:** Move from detachment to compassionate presence.
*   **Tool:** *The Holding Space* (Active listening practice).
*   **Glossary Lens:** Focus on "Presence," "Observation," "Stillness."

Add these terms to the site glossary, tagged with their relevant archetypes.

*   **Threshold (Invitation):** The energetic boundary between self and other; the place of choice.
*   **Calcification (Stone Breaker):** When a stone (habit/pattern) hardens and loses its life force, requiring breaking.
*   **Summoning (Stone Caller):** The often unconscious act of drawing obligations to oneself through language or desire.
*   **The Archive (Stone Keeper):** The collective weight of ancestral and cultural stones carried by a lineage.
*   **Anchoring (Stone Witness):** The ability to remain immovable in one's own frequency amidst external chaos.
